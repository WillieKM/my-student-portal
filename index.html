Our Programs

Each program combines biblical teaching, theological depth, and practical ministry training to prepare you for Kingdom service.

Certificate in Biblical Studies

Foundational Scripture study in hermeneutics, Old and New Testament, and Christian doctrine for personal growth and ministry service.

Diploma in Theology

Systematic and biblical theology, church history, and doctrine to equip students to teach and lead with theological clarity.

Diploma in Biblical Studies

In-depth biblical interpretation and applied ministry training for those called to deeper Scripture engagement in church contexts.

Bachelor's Degree in Theology

A comprehensive undergraduate program covering theology, biblical studies, ethics, and leadership for pastoral ministry and global service.

Master's Degree in Theology

Graduate-level formation covering advanced biblical studies, homiletics, and ministry strategy for experienced ministry leaders.

Doctorate Degree in Divinity

The highest level of theological training, preparing scholars and ministers for advanced research, teaching, and apostolic leadership.
