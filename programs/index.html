Our Programs

Certificate in Biblical Studies

An entry-level program covering the foundations of Old and New Testament Scripture, basic hermeneutics, and Christian doctrine. Ideal for believers seeking a solid biblical foundation for personal growth and ministry service.

Diploma in Theology

A focused study of systematic and biblical theology, church history, and Christian doctrine. Equips students to teach, preach, and lead with theological clarity, integrity, and conviction.

Diploma in Biblical Studies

An in-depth exploration of biblical interpretation, Old and New Testament surveys, and applied ministry. Designed for those called to deeper engagement with Scripture in local church and mission contexts.

Bachelor's Degree in Theology

A comprehensive undergraduate program integrating theology, biblical studies, church history, ethics, and Christian leadership. Prepares graduates for pastoral ministry, missions, and broad Kingdom service.

Master's Degree in Theology

Graduate-level formation for ministry leaders seeking academic depth and pastoral excellence. Covers advanced biblical studies, homiletics, apologetics, and strategic ministry leadership.

Doctorate Degree in Divinity

The highest level of theological training at Revealed Bible College. Designed for experienced ministers and scholars pursuing advanced research, teaching, and apostolic leadership in the global Church.
